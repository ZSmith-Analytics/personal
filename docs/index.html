About

Public Service

City of Beacon · Ward 2

Councilman Zack Smith

Sworn in on January 4, 2026. I ran because Beacon’s quality of life can be improved with data, research, and attention to residents who feel overlooked as the city redevelops.

“As a data scientist, I am able to use data and research to make the best-informed decisions for our city, which is especially important with big decisions coming for zoning and redevelopment projects.”

Priorities I have raised with Ward 2 residents include holding short-term-rental landlords accountable, working with Main Street landlords on weekend parking, and enforcing quality-of-life rules — parking, leashes, noise — so Beacon stays orderly as it grows.

City Council meetings and workshops are streamed and archived on the City of Beacon YouTube channel. Follow along there, then write me at zsmith@beaconny.gov if you have a Ward 2 or city issue.

Data Science

Uber · New York

Senior Data Scientist

I run experiments to improve the onboarding funnel for Earners — the people who drive, deliver, and make the marketplace work. The job is measurement with consequences: design the test, ship the analysis, and recommend the product change.

Day to day that looks like A/B tests, switchbacks, and synthetic controls; Python notebooks end-to-end; Hive and Spark for scheduled datasets; Presto SQL for analysis; and dashboards in Google Data Studio for engineering, product, and senior leadership.

Code and one-off analytics live on GitHub.
